What this is

Routeloop is a tool for planning, organizing and sharing road trips. It is not a navigation system and must not be relied on as one. It is currently in a closed alpha, which means accounts are approved by hand and things will break.

You need an account to plan a ride, and accounts are approved individually. Keep your sign-in to yourself; anything done through your account is treated as done by you. Tell me if you think someone else has got into it.

Pick a username that is yours to pick. Impersonating someone else, or claiming a handle to sit on it, is grounds for it being taken back.

Your content

Your rides are yours. Planning a ride here does not give me any ownership of it. What you upload, type and plan stays yours.

You give me only the permission needed to run the service: to store your content, and to show it to you and to anyone you share a link with. That permission ends when you delete the content or the account.

Do not upload anything you do not have the right to upload, and do not use Routeloop to store or share anything unlawful.

Riding is your responsibility

This is the part that actually matters on a motorcycle.

Routes are computed from map data that is sometimes wrong, sometimes out of date, and never aware of what the road is doing today. Roads close. Bridges wash out. A track that looks paved on a map can be gravel, or private, or gone. Distances and times are estimates, and estimates for a car at that.

Judgment on the road is yours alone. Check conditions before you go, ride within the law and your ability, and do not follow a line off a screen into something you can see is a bad idea. Routeloop plans rides; it does not know what the weather is doing or what your tires can take.

Fair use

Do not scrape the site, hammer the API, or use Routeloop in a way that degrades it for anyone else. The limits in the app—routes per ride, stops per route—are there to keep it standing, not to be worked around.
